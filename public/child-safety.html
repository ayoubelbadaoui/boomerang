Child Safety Standards

Last updated: June 25, 2026

Boomerang has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We are committed to keeping minors safe and to preventing, detecting, and removing any content or behavior that sexually exploits or endangers children. This page describes our standards and how we comply with applicable child safety laws.

Our zero-tolerance policy

  • CSAM and any content that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers minors is strictly prohibited.
  • Grooming, sextortion, trafficking, and any sexualization of minors are prohibited.
  • Accounts that create, share, or solicit such content are permanently banned.
  • The app is not directed to children and requires users to be of the minimum age permitted in their region.

How we prevent and respond

  • Users can report any post, profile, or message directly in the app, with an option to block the offending account.
  • We review reports of CSAE and act promptly to remove violating content and disable offending accounts.
  • We preserve relevant information and report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the appropriate regional and national authorities, as required by law.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement investigations relating to child safety.

How to report a child safety concern

Inside the app: use the Report option on any post, profile, or message and select the relevant reason. You can also block the account.

By email, contact our child safety point of contact: bmg.founder@icloud.com

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

Reporting CSAM to authorities

In the United States, suspected CSAM can be reported to the NCMEC CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org. Outside the United States, please report to your local hotline or law enforcement (for example, a member hotline of the International Association of Internet Hotlines, INHOPE).

Compliance

Boomerang complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations, including requirements to report apparent CSAM to the relevant regional and national authorities. We continually review and improve our safety practices.
