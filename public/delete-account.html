Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Boomerang account and all associated data at any time.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open the Boomerang app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Manage Accounts.
  3. Tap Delete Account under "Account Control".
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are permanently removed.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address linked to your account and we'll delete it for you:

Email: azze05@icloud.com

We process deletion requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the following:

  • Your profile information (name, email, date of birth, photo)
  • Your posts, videos, and uploaded media
  • Your comments, reactions, and messages
  • Your followers, following, and saved content
  • Your authentication credentials

Data retention

Data typeRetention after deletion
Account & profile dataDeleted immediately
User-generated contentDeleted immediately
Backups & logsPurged within 30 days
Records required by lawRetained only as legally required

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
