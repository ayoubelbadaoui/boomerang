Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?

Open the app and sign up with your email. Once verified, set up your profile and you're ready to start sharing.

How do I post a video?

Tap the record button, capture or pick a short clip, and share it to your feed. Your followers will see it instantly.

How do I report content or a user?

Use the options menu on any post or profile to report content that violates our guidelines. We review every report.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account, or email us and we'll remove your account and associated data.
